Help Desk & Frequently Asked Questions

General Inquiries

Click on the Lodge Complaint tab in the navigation header. Fill in your complainant details, select the appropriate department category, select the urgency level, describe your grievance in detail, and optionally attach photos or PDF receipts. Upon submission, an official Complaint ID (e.g. CMP-2026-XXXXX) and scannable QR Code will be generated.

Navigate to the Track Status page and enter your Complaint ID. You will be able to see the interactive 4-stage progress stepper (Submitted → Under Review → In Progress → Resolved), the assigned officer's name, and live timeline remarks.

Yes! Use the My Complaints page. Simply enter your registered email address or 10-digit mobile number to see your complete grievance history, filter by status, and click through to any case.

You can upload image files (JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP) or document files (PDF) up to a maximum size of 5 MB. The system automatically inspects file headers and MIME types to prevent malicious uploads.

Once an officer marks your grievance as Resolved, an interactive 5-star rating widget becomes available on your tracking page. You can rate the quality of service from 1 to 5 stars and leave remarks.
