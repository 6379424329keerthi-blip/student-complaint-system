How The Redressal System Works

A streamlined path from issue reporting to verified resolution with zero bureaucracy.

1. Register Complaint

Fill out the structured complaint form with category, location, and optional photo/document evidence. Receive a unique Tracking ID and scannable QR Code instantly.

2. Department Action

The grievance is automatically routed to designated departmental officers. Live stage updates (Under Review, Assigned, In Progress) are logged with audit timestamps.

3. Verified Resolution

Once repairs or actions are completed, officers submit resolution remarks. Complainants rate the resolution quality and provide feedback to ensure service excellence.
